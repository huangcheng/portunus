Portunus privacy notice

Portunus at portunus.wuhan.dev is a private gateway. This notice covers the Google and Baidu Netdisk accounts you connect to it.

What Google access is requested

If you connect Google Drive, the app asks to view files in your Drive and to create or change files that the app opens or creates.

How that access is used

The gateway stores the OAuth access token and refresh token on the operator’s server and uses them to call Google Drive for agents that operator has authorized. Drive contents are not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

How long it is kept

Stored tokens are kept until you disconnect Google Drive in Portunus, at which point they are deleted. Revoking the app in your Google Account stops further access immediately, but does not by itself remove tokens already stored on the operator’s server; disconnect in Portunus to remove them.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Portunus’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Baidu Netdisk

If you connect Baidu Netdisk (百度网盘), the operator’s server keeps the access token issued by Baidu, in the app’s state directory, and runs the official bdpan command-line tool with it to list, search, upload or download files for agents the operator has authorized. Baidu Netdisk file contents are not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else. Tokens are kept until you disconnect the app in Portunus.
